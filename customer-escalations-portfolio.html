This portfolio documents named enterprise customers whose production issues I directly investigated, root-caused, and resolved as a storage and compute engineer on IBM PowerVC — an OpenStack-based private cloud platform for IBM Power Systems.

A "customer escalation" in this context means the issue was reported from a live production environment, filed through IBM's enterprise support system, and assigned to the engineering team for a code-level fix or targeted patch. Many were P1 (critical production outage) or P2 (major function impaired) severity — requiring fast turnaround and direct customer communication. Customer identities have been generalised to protect confidentiality while preserving the industry, geography, and scale context.
